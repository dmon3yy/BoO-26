# Notes on *The Battle of Olympus* (NES)

Our own summary of how the original plays, written to guide homage — **mechanics only**. No maps, sprites, text or music from the original are used anywhere in this project (see `../IP_RULES.md`).

**Sources consulted (October 2026):** the Pixel Guides walkthrough and its reference pages (route, items, bosses, enemies, controls, checklist, FAQ, secrets) at pixelguides.org/the-battle-of-olympus/, cross-checked with Mike's RPG Center and the RPGClassics shrine. The guide rates its own names and costs as medium confidence; small errors don't matter for allusion.

## Shape of the game
- Ten areas: Arcadia, Attica, Argolis, Peloponnesus, Laconia, Phthia, Crete, Phrygia, Tartarus, the Palace of Hades.
- Non-linear, with a common "stable" route: Arcadia → Attica → Argolis → Peloponnesus → Laconia → back through Argolis and Attica → Phthia → Crete → Phrygia → Tartarus → Palace. Areas are revisited once new items reveal entrances.
- Olives (dropped by enemies) are the currency; gods and a few vendors charge for help.

## Controls and continuation
- D-pad moves, climbs, enters doors, crouches; A jumps (hold for height); B attacks and talks; Start opens items; Select uses the special item. Crouch over a dropped item to pick it up.
- Gods in temples give a password, the "Word of the Gods". After game over, *Continue* restarts the area with olives halved; *Retry* returns to the last temple that gave a Word, keeping items.

## Items, in route order (as remembered by the guide)
- **Club** (start). **Staff of Fennel** — from a boy rescued from Lamia in Attica; Prometheus in Argolis gives it fire (lights caves, burns thorns).
- **Sandals of Hermes** — meet Hermes in Argolis, receive them back at Zeus's altar in Arcadia; walk on ceilings.
- **Shield of Athena** — blocks arrows and projectiles.
- **Harp of Apollo** (Peloponnesus) — counters the Siren; summons Pegasus at sun monuments.
- **Sword** — from a nymph after the Hydra; the only thing that hurts the Cyclops's eye.
- **Ocarina** (Poseidon, Laconia, ~60 olives) — summons dolphins; wakes Gaea, the boulder blocking Phthia.
- **Eye of the Graeae** — from the three Graeae; reveals the two hidden nymph doors.
- **Key** — given in Athens once you show the first Fragment.
- **Divine Sword** (Hephaestus, Phthia, ~70 olives) — fires a beam (the "Power of Argus") at full health.
- **Power Bracelet** (Ares, Crete, 80 olives) — doubles attack; removes the beam's health cost. *Trap:* accept with less than 80 olives and Ares takes your Sandals; you must go back to Hermes and then Zeus.
- **Salamander Shield** (Circe, Crete) — 20 salamander skins (from a nest in Argolis) plus olives; blocks fire.
- **Moon Crystal** (Artemis, Phrygia) — needed to find Hades in the dark.
- **Fragments of Love** ×3 — nymphs in Argolis (Eye), Phthia (Eye + upper ledge + Key) and Crete (behind a barrier opened by the first two, past the Minotaur). They restore Helene at the end.
- **Ambrosia** — seven fixed places, each extends and refills health; one hides in a Peloponnesus tree. **Golden Apple** — optional, from Ladon; permanently halves damage. **Nectar / pitcher** — carried full heal.

## Bosses (16 types)
Colchis Bull (Arcadia, recurring), Lamia (Attica archer), Nemean Lion (guards Prometheus), Hydra (head only hittable when surfaced), Cyclops (Laconia gate), Siren (Island of Ruins), the Graeae (shared eye), Gaea (Phthia gate), Centaur (optional), Ladon (optional; Golden Apple), Talos (Crete gate), Minotaur (Crete maze), Dark Pegasus (Phrygia), Gold Cyclops (wrong Tartarus door), Cerberus (two regrowing heads), Hades (mirrors Orpheus in the dark).

## Tartarus and the Palace
- Four stacked entrance rooms with one correct door sequence; wrong doors force stronger repeat fights.
- One-way exits throw you back to Argolis or into long loops.
- The Palace opens with a stronger Lamia; a false staircase leads to a nymph who heals you and sends you back to the entrance; Helene's statue stands near the dark room where Hades waits.
