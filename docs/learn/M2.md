# M2 — How this works

**TL;DR:** M2 adds fighting and music that matters. Orpheus swings a club, dodges, parries and plays his lyre. Enemies **move freely but attack on the beat**, so the music tells you when to dodge or parry. Doing things on the beat is never required, but it pays: harder hits, perfect parries and Muse, the meter that powers combat songs. As in M1, nearly every number is in a text file in `content/`.

## The kit

All of these numbers are in `content/combat.json`. The code that uses them is `game/actors/player/kit.gd`.

| Move | Key | What it does |
|---|---|---|
| **Club** | X | An overhead swing. It has three phases. *Startup* (0.06 s) is the wind-up, when it can't hit yet. *Active* (0.10 s) is when the club can hit. *Recovery* (0.14 s) is when you can't act yet. The three phases are what make a swing feel weighty instead of instant. |
| **Dodge** | C | A short dash. You can't be hurt during it. You get one dodge in the air per jump. |
| **Parry** | V | Raises your guard for 0.2 s. It blocks anything coming **from the front**. |
| **Hurt** | — | You lose health and get knocked back. Then you blink for 1 s, and nothing can hurt you while you blink. |

You have 12 HP. Each red pip on the HUD is 2 HP. If you run out, you die exactly as in M1: you drop half your olives and wake at your Hearth. Resting at a Hearth also restores full health.

**Hitstop** (from M1) now fires on every hit. It freezes the game for a few hundredths of a second, and longer on an on-beat hit. That freeze is a large part of why a hit feels solid.

## The beat bonus

When you swing, parry or play a note, the kit asks the conductor (`Music.grade_now()`) how close you were to a beat:

- **PERFECT:** within 50 ms of the beat.
- **GOOD:** within 100 ms.
- **MISS:** anything further off.

GOOD or PERFECT counts as **on the beat**, and earns:
- **Club:** ×1.5 damage, a gold burst, and a bright ping on top of the thud. You hear the bonus.
- **Parry:** a *perfect parry*. The attacker staggers for 1.2 s, and venom flies back at whatever spat it.
- **Muse:** +8 for an on-beat hit, +15 for a perfect parry.

The grade shows top right, next to the beat dot that pulses with the music.

## Enemies that keep time

The enemy code is in `game/actors/enemies/`, and their numbers are in `content/enemies.json`. Every enemy hears `_on_beat()` once per beat. It starts its wind-up on one beat and **strikes on a later one**, which gives you a beat of warning.

- **Satyr** (`s` in a room file): paces its patch of ground. When it sees you, it rears up on one beat and charges on the next. Dodge through it on that next beat, or parry it on the beat.
- **Bat** (`v`): hangs from the ceiling. When you come close, it drops on a beat and swoops through where you were standing, arriving exactly one beat later.
- **Snake** (`n`): rears back on one beat and spits on the next. The venom is aimed to reach you **on the following beat**, so a parry on that beat is always a perfect one and sends the venom back.

Getting hit, staggered or charmed cancels whatever an enemy was doing. Rooms rebuild their enemies when you come back, as in classic games.

## The lyre and songs

Hold **Shift** and Orpheus stops to play. While you hold it, Up, Down, Left and Right pluck four strings, and Jump plucks the middle one. The notes you play appear above his head, gold if they were on the beat. When your last few notes spell a song he knows, the song plays. The songs are listed in `content/songs.json`:

| Song | Notes | Kind | Effect |
|---|---|---|---|
| Song of Zeus | ↑ ← → ↑ | traversal (free) | Opens song seals within reach. Try it at the glowing seal in the temple (room 2). |
| Song of Charming | ↓ ← ↓ → | combat, 50 Muse | Enemies nearby sway, harmless, for 4 s. Orpheus charming wild beasts is straight out of the myth. |
| Calliope's Hymn | ← → • ↑ | combat, 40 Muse | Heals 4 HP. |

A combat song played **entirely on the beat costs half**. An opened seal stays open: it is saved, and the Word remembers it too.

For M2 only, Orpheus starts with the lyre, the club, the shield and all three songs (`content/new_game.json`), so you can try everything anywhere. In M3, Zeus hands them out properly.

## Music that follows the fight

Each song comes in three stems: melody, harmony and percussion. They all play at once, perfectly in sync, and `Music.mix()` fades each one up or down:
- While you're exploring, the drums sit low, at 35%.
- When an enemy comes within 180 px, the drums come in. The music tells you a fight is on, and gives you a clear beat to fight to.
- While you play the lyre, the melody steps back so your notes carry the tune.

## Pause, calibration and comfort

**Esc** (or Enter, or Start) opens the pause menu:
- **Rhythm assist:** widens the beat windows by 60%.
- **Input offset:** nudges your timing in 5 ms steps.
- **Calibrate the beat:** opens the Beat Lab from M0. Tap along for 16 beats and it measures your device's delay, so "on the beat" means on the beat for you. This matters on Bluetooth headphones.
- **Screen shake:** a small shake when you're hurt. You can turn it off.

| Combat sounds | `tools/build_audio.py` (`combat_sfx`) |

The enemy art uses the same rig system as Orpheus. A rig part can now be shown only in some animations (`"only": ["attack"]`), which is how the club appears just for the swing.

**Tests:** `tests/unit/test_combat.gd` builds a tiny arena with one floor and checks the claims in this document. On-beat hits do 1.5× damage. The club hits what's in front and misses what's behind. Dodging can't be hurt. A parry blocks from the front only, and an on-beat parry staggers. Charmed enemies are harmless. The hymn heals for Muse.

## Try it yourself

1. **Make the satyr hit harder.** In `content/enemies.json`, set the satyr's `contact_damage` to `4`. Each charge now takes two pips.
2. **Give the satyr a longer warning.** Set `windup_beats` to `2`. It now rears up for two beats before it charges. That's a good setting for an easier region.
3. **Write a song.** Add one to `content/songs.json`, for example `"notes": ["up", "up", "down", "down"]` with `"effect": "heal"`, `"kind": "combat"`, `"cost": 20` and `"amount": 2`. Then add `"song:<your id>"` to `content/new_game.json`. Play it in the game.
4. **Feel the windows.** Turn on rhythm assist in the pause menu and compare how often you get PERFECT.

## Key takeaways
- Each attack has startup, active and recovery phases, and that split is what makes a swing feel heavy.
- Enemies attack on the beat. That turns the music into a warning system, and the beat bonus turns it into a skill you can master.
- Muse is earned by playing in time and spent on combat songs.
- Layered stems let one song score both exploring and fighting.
