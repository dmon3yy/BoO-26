# M3 — How this works

**TL;DR:** M3 is the vertical slice: Arcadia, start to finish, in about 20–30 minutes. Orpheus starts with nothing. He finds a club, climbs to Zeus, gets his lyre restrung and learns a song. That song opens a sealed temple, which holds the Hymn. Then he goes down through the caves to the Colchis Bull, and on to the road east. Hermes narrates as you go, and people talk to you. A journal keeps track of what you've learned. Death now costs something: half your olives stay where you fell, and you have to go back for them. As before, nearly everything you might want to tune is in a text file in `content/`.

The question for this milestone: **is this the game?**

## The route

| # | Room | What happens |
|---|---|---|
| 1 | The Frozen Grove | You wake by Eurydice's fire. The club lies under a frozen olive tree. |
| 2 | The Temple Steps | The temple door is sealed with a song you don't know yet. Climb over the roof. |
| 3 | Shepherd's Drop, The Frozen Brook | Satyrs, bats and snakes, plus your first real fights. |
| 4 | Hermit's Climb | A Hearth, then a long climb. |
| 5 | Zeus's Altar | Zeus is bored. He restrings your lyre and teaches you the **Song of Zeus**. He also sells **Ambrosia** (+1 heart) for 30 olives. |
| 6 | Back at the Temple Steps | Play the Song of Zeus at the seal. The door opens. |
| 7 | The Temple Crypt | A spike pit, then **Calliope's Hymn** on the altar. |
| 8 | The Olive Caves, The Deep Caves | Scorpions in a low tunnel. A free Ambrosia waits at the far end. |
| 9 | The Bull's Pen | The guardian. Beating it opens the bronze gate. |
| 10 | The Road East | Hermes is waiting where the road runs out. The slice ends here. |

## Starting with nothing

A new game hands you nothing (`content/new_game.json` has an empty `abilities` list). Everything you have, you picked up or were given:

- **The club** is a pickup in the Frozen Grove: `k = pickup ability=club` in the room file.
- **The lyre and the Song of Zeus** come from Zeus. His entry in `content/npcs.json` lists what he grants, and his dialogue calls `World.restring_lyre()`.
- **The Hymn** is a pickup in the crypt. Play it with Shift held: Left, Right, Jump (the middle string), Up.

`GameState.has(need)` is the one question the whole game asks: "do I have this ability, or is this flag raised?" Gates, seals, journal entries and dialogue branches all ask it.

## People and dialogue

Zeus, Eurydice and Hermes are **NPCs**. Each has an entry in `content/npcs.json` with its rig, its dialogue file, an optional light, and `titles`: which part of the conversation to start from, depending on what you've done. Walk up and press **Up** to talk.

While you talk, the world pauses, so a satyr can't hit you mid-sentence. The dialogue box moves to the top of the screen when the speakers stand low, so it never covers them.

The conversations are in `content/dialogue/*.dialogue` (Dialogue Manager format). Lines can change the game: `do World.buy_ambrosia(30)` spends olives, and `set GameState.flags["met_zeus"] = true` remembers you met him. A reply can be hidden behind a condition, for example `[if not GameState.get_flag("bought_ambrosia")]`.

## Hermes and the journal

**Hermes** narrates in captions across the top of the screen. They type themselves out and move on by themselves, and the game never stops for him. Each line is spoken once (remembered in `GameState.told`). His lines are in `content/narration.json`, keyed by what triggers them:

- `room:arcadia_07`: first time you enter a room
- `ability:club`, `flag:beat_bull`: when you gain something
- `death:1`, `death:4`: your first and fourth deaths
- `hearth`, `bull_wakes`, `olives_back`

The **journal** is in the pause menu. The first page lists your songs with their notes drawn as arrows. After that come entries from `content/journal.json`, each unlocked by a `when` (`flag:met_zeus`, `ability:club`, `room:arcadia_08` or `always`). Nothing extra is saved, because the journal is worked out from the game state. That means a Word restores it too.

## Olives, death and enemies coming back

Olives are the currency. Some lie around as pickups, and enemies drop them when they die (the `olives` number per enemy in `content/enemies.json`). They scatter, then drift toward you when you're close.

When you die, **half your olives (rounded up) stay where you last stood on solid ground**. They sit in a glowing pile. Walk onto it and they're yours again; nothing guards it. If you die before you get there, the old pile is gone and a new one takes its place.

**Enemies you beat stay beaten until you rest at a Hearth or die.** Then they all come back. Wandering away and returning doesn't bring them back. Guardians like the Bull never come back. The game keeps the list in `GameState.slain`, and each enemy is named by its room and the tile where it starts.

## The crouch strike and scorpions

Hold **Down** to crouch. That makes you a smaller target, and an attack while crouched is a **low sweep** (`club_low` in `content/combat.json`).

Scorpions are too low for a standing swing to touch, so the crouch strike is how you fight them. They creep along, raise their tail for a beat, then sting a short way forward.

## The Colchis Bull

The Bull sleeps until you get close. Then it roars and starts its pattern:

1. **Paw** on one beat, **charge** on the next.
2. If it runs into a wall, it is **dazed** for a few beats. That's your opening.
3. Its **front is bronze armour**. Hits from the front just clank. Hit it from behind, or anywhere while it's dazed.
4. Below half health it gets angry: it **inhales**, then breathes a low jet of flame. Jump it.

Its numbers are in `content/enemies.json` under `colchis_bull`: `hp` 24, `charge_speed`, `stun_beats`, `flame_from_hp`. Beating it raises the flag `beat_bull`. The gate's `needs=beat_bull` sees that flag, and the gate lifts.

## Gates, seals and the world checker

A **gate** (`g = gate needs=...`) is a solid door that opens itself once the need is met. A **song seal** (`S = song_seal song=zeus`) opens when you play that song next to it.

`tools/validate_world.py` now understands both. It plays the game in rounds: "with what I can reach, what can I pick up, who will give me something, and which guardian can I beat?" It repeats until nothing new turns up. Then it complains if anything that matters can't be reached, or if any spot is a dead end you can't get back out of.

**Tests:** `tests/unit/test_slice.gd` checks the claims in this document:
- A new game starts empty-handed.
- Walking into the club gives you the club.
- The pen gate opens when the Bull is beaten, and a beaten Bull stays beaten.
- Dying leaves half your olives where you fell, and walking back to them gets them all back.
- Beaten enemies stay down until you rest or die.
- Enter, Space or Z answers in every conversation (`tests/unit/test_talk.gd`).
- Zeus restrings the lyre, and Ambrosia costs olives and adds a heart.
- The journal grows as you play.
- A standing swing misses a scorpion while a crouch strike lands.
- The Bull's front is armour.

## Try it yourself

1. **Make death gentler.** In `game/autoload/game_state.gd`, `drop_olives_on_death` drops `ceili(olives / 2.0)`. Make it `/ 4.0` and die on purpose in the crypt's spike pit.
2. **Make Ambrosia cheaper.** In `content/dialogue/zeus.dialogue`, change both `30`s to `15`.
3. **Give Hermes a new line.** Add `"room:arcadia_04": ["Thin ice. Watch the east end."]` to `content/narration.json`. Walk into the Frozen Brook.
4. **Make the Bull meaner.** Set `stun_beats` to `2` and `flame_from_hp` to `18`. You get less time to punish it, and the flame comes sooner.

## Key takeaways
- The slice's whole progression is one question, `GameState.has(need)`, asked by pickups, people, gates, seals, dialogue and the journal.
- Content drives the story: rooms, people, lines and journal entries are all text files, and the world checker proves the route can be finished.
- Death costs something without wiping you out: half your olives, plus the walk back to get them.
- The Bull teaches one idea (get behind it, or make it hit a wall), then raises the pressure with the flame.
