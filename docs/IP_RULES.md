# IP rules

*Ballad of Orpheus* honours two works it does not own: **The Battle of Olympus** (Infinity / Imagineer / Brøderbund, 1988–89) and the musical **Hadestown** (Anaïs Mitchell). These rules keep everything we make ours.

> Not legal advice. Before any commercial release, have an IP attorney review the game, its title and its store page.

## Never copy

| From | Never use |
|---|---|
| *Hadestown* | Lyrics, song titles, melodies, dialogue, character or costume designs, staging, the name "Hadestown", the train motif (we use Charon's steam ferry) |
| *The Battle of Olympus* | Sprites, tiles, music, sound effects, maps or room layouts, text, logo, the title, the original passwords |
| Anyone else | Assets, fonts, samples or code we don't have the rights to |

Room layouts are **never traced** from the original's maps, including the StrategyWiki maps the reference guide links to. The guide is for remembering *mechanics*, not geometry.

## Free to use

- **Greek myth (public domain):** every name (Orpheus, Eurydice, Hermes, Hades, Persephone, the Fates, Charon, Zeus, Athena, Apollo, Artemis, Hephaestus, Prometheus, Ares, Circe, Ladon, the Graeae, Cerberus, Talos, the Minotaur, the Colchis Bulls, Lycaon…), every place (Arcadia, Attica, Argolis, Laconia, Peloponnese, Phthia, Phrygia, Crete, Taenarum, Tartarus, Asphodel, the Styx, Erebos), and every mythological object (Staff of Fennel, Harp of Apollo, Golden Apple, Sandals of Hermes, Eye of the Graeae, ambrosia, nectar).
- **Genres and techniques:** folk, blues, swing, chiptune, Greek modes, Metroidvania structure, rhythm combat.

## Allowed as allusion (in our own words, art and code)

- **From the original game:** olives as currency; seven Ambrosia and the tree that hides one; the Salamander Shield (20 skins); the Sandals' ceiling-walk and the relay quest for them; the Eye revealing hidden doors; three nymph-guarded fragments becoming verses, with the Key and two-fragment barrier chain; greedy gods; Ares's repossession trap; the Divine Sword's full-health beam; the crouch strike; Words of the Gods; Pegasus at Sun Monuments; dolphin sea lanes; Gaea woken by music; the Siren countered by the Harp; the colour-coded Cretan labyrinth; a Tartarus door riddle (with a *different* sequence); Cerberus's regrowing heads; the false staircase; loop doors; the mirror Hades revealed in moonlit water; Helene and her statue.
- **From Hadestown, only as broad ideas:** an industrial underworld, a wall, labour and lost names, broken seasons, a messenger narrator, a trio of Fates, a deal, a walk out where looking back loses everything. Character designs follow **mythology**, not the stage show.

## Practical checks

1. New music: compose from scratch in `content/music/*.score`; never transcribe or "approximate" a melody you remember.
2. New text: if a line sounds like a lyric you've heard, rewrite it.
3. New art: build from our palettes and generators, or draw it fresh; no tracing references.
4. Credits list every third-party tool and licence (Godot, GUT, Dialogue Manager are MIT).
